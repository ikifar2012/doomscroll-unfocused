Keep playing.
Switch away.

A small Chrome extension that helps videos keep playing when you switch tabs or windows.

Instagram and CNN are included. Add other sites when you need them, and control each one from the toolbar.

Download for ChromeHow to install

Free download. Chrome 119+. Install as an unpacked extension.

Install it in Chrome.

Download the ZIP above, then:

  1. Extract the ZIP into a folder you’ll keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and enable Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the folder containing manifest.json.
  4. Refresh your video tab, start playback, and switch away.

For updates, replace the files, reload the extension, and refresh your tabs.

Your sites. Your choice.

Turn sites on or off from the popup. Adding a new domain asks Chrome for access to that domain. Refresh open tabs after changing settings.

Local by design.

Your site preferences stay on your device. The extension has no analytics, accounts, or external services. Read the privacy policy.

What about scrolling on X?

Add X and opt into Keep playing while scrolling. It’s off by default. It blocks scroll-triggered pauses while the player exists, but cannot keep playback alive after X removes the video or clears its source.

A little less interruption.

This extension adjusts the focus and visibility signals sites receive. It doesn’t force autoplay, prevent Chrome from suspending tabs, or guarantee compatibility with every player. Report a problem if a site behaves differently.
